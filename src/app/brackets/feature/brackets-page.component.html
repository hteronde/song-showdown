 BRACKET SEEDING

How seeds work

Seeds rank songs to help avoid eliminating big contenders early. A #1 seed is ranked higher than a #2 seed.

Choose a seed range, then assign a seed to every song. Each seed must have the same number of songs. For example, a 16-song bracket with a range of 4 has four #1 seeds, four #2 seeds, four #3 seeds, and four #4 seeds.

Randomizing songs within each seed

When Use Seeds and Randomize Songs are both selected, songs are shuffled within their seed groups: the four #1 seeds are randomized among themselves, then the four #2 seeds, and so on. Songs keep their assigned seed.

The opening matchups pair the highest seeds against the lowest seeds. With seeds 1–8, the pairings are 1 vs. 8, 2 vs. 7, 3 vs. 6, and 4 vs. 5. Higher seeds are spread across the bracket. If you choose Randomize Each Round, advancing songs are shuffled within equal seed groups and the highest remaining seeds face the lowest remaining seeds.

Using seeds to break ties

Seeds can also decide a tied vote automatically. In Tiebreaker option, choose Seed (higher seed wins, #1 beats #2) or Seed (lower seed wins, #2 beats #1). If both songs have the same seed, a random draw breaks the tie. You can still choose Random or Admin pick instead.

If you aren’t randomizing songs and don’t want to use seeds as tiebreakers, you probably don’t need to use seeds.
